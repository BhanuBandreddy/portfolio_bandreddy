## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: enterprise buyers in financial services (insurance, banking, NBFC) and the procurement and transformation leaders around them, deciding whether to brief Bhanu Harish Bandreddy and his team on a transformation mandate. Secondary: recruiters and hiring leaders for VP-level product, solution-architecture and AI roles. Buyers come first; the site serves both without splitting into two sites.

## Product Purpose

A portfolio site for Bhanu Harish Bandreddy, VP, Product Innovation & AI Solutions (Chennai), and the pods of teams he leads. It shows how enterprise CRM, insurance distribution and agentic-AI platforms get built and scaled across markets, and it gives a visitor a clear path to start a conversation. Success: a qualified buyer sends a brief or books a call; a recruiter requests an interview.

## Positioning

Compounding AI workflows delivered by pods of teams, kept inside governed architecture: deterministic rules (eligibility, pricing, approval, accounting) stay source-backed and testable while AI handles interpretation, extraction and generation. The seven-stage Enterprise AI Delivery Framework is the owned method behind it.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive from a referral, RFP or LinkedIn and skim before deciding. Evidence is shown through working architecture walkthroughs, nine case studies and an interactive seven-stage framework map (/approach/framework). Contact is by email (hareesh.b3@gmail.com) and LinkedIn.

## Product Principles

1. Proof before promise: lead with working walkthroughs and real architecture, not claims.
2. Governed AI: show where AI judgement stops and deterministic, testable rules begin.
3. A team effort with a named lead: the voice is "we", the accountability is Bhanu's.
4. One clear next step on every page: a visitor can always start a conversation.
5. Plain, specific language: operational detail over buzzwords.
